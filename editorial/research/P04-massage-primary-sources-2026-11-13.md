# P04 · Massage guide — verified source dossier (DRAFT, not QA-approved)
Checked 2026-11-13, Europe/Bucharest. Internal editorial research only; not a published guide.

## Verified Romanian primary sources
1. Colegiul Fizioterapeuților din România (CFZRO), https://colegiulfizioterapeutilor.ro/ — professional regulatory body. Its homepage directs readers to check the *Registrul unic al fizioterapeuților* and states that active registration is required to practice physiotherapy legally in Romania. Do not conflate all relaxation massage providers with regulated physiotherapists.
2. Public CFZRO register, https://tm-c.eu/CFZRO/RegNational — searchable by name, NRU, active/inactive status and territorial college (including București–Ilfov). The registry shows no results until filters are applied; no individual practitioner was verified in this research.
3. CFZRO document validity lookup, https://tm-c.eu/CFZRO/ValDocumente — checks authorization/annual endorsement numbers; no individual documents were verified.
4. Official Romanian legislation, Law 229/2016, https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/216496 — regulates the physiotherapy profession, not every commercial relaxation massage.
5. Official implementing rules, https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/221001 — explains the national register and patient informed consent in physiotherapy.

## Actionable reader value to include after independent QA
- Distinguish *masaj de relaxare* (wellness) from medical *fizioterapie/kinetoterapie* and ask what exact service is offered.
- For physiotherapy, request the specialist's full name and NRU, verify active status in the official register and document validity where applicable.
- Ask the provider directly about session duration, total price, cancellation rules, language, address and accessibility; do not invent current prices.
- Do not present massage as a replacement for assessment of acute injury, severe pain or neurological symptoms.
