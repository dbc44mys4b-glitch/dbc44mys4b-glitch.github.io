# BucharestUA · Editorial standards, corrections and disclosures
Updated: 2026-11-13. This is the editorial operating policy, not a claim of independent certification.

## Editorial responsibility
BucharestUA publishes practical guides for residents and newcomers to Bucharest. Content is maintained by the BucharestUA editorial project; no individual medical, legal or financial credentials are claimed. Editorial assistance may include AI drafting, but every material factual claim requires human-verifiable source evidence and an independent editorial QA record before new publication.

## Source standard
Prefer Romanian official authorities, hospitals, transport operators, educational institutions and the primary service provider's own current page. For each changing fact (price, address, hours, eligibility, schedule), retain the source URL and date checked. A source link does not imply endorsement. If primary evidence is unavailable, remove the specific claim or clearly state what remains unverified; do not invent an expert or testimonial.

## Corrections and freshness
When an error is reported or discovered, record the article URL, exact claim, evidence URL, date, severity, and proposed correction in the editorial issue log. Safety-critical inaccuracies (emergency, health, immigration) require immediate review; where prompt correction cannot be verified, visibly flag or withdraw the affected passage. Corrections must preserve the article's original publication date, update dateModified only when content actually changes, and record what changed. A modified article must receive a new independent QA decision for its exact content hash before it is counted as approved.

## Images and rights
Use original commissioned/generated assets with recorded provenance or appropriately licensed photographs with documented license terms, attribution where required, and alt text. A generic brand OG image is not a substitute for a relevant, rights-cleared article illustration. If rights or topical match cannot be established, the article fails the visual gate.

## Commercial independence
Paid placements, sponsorships, and affiliate links must be labeled plainly near the relevant recommendation. No fabricated reviews, invented usage experience, unverified rankings, paid link spam or guaranteed results. Community recommendations are unverified personal experiences, not endorsements or professional referrals.

## Community and privacy
Telegram community discussions are not a substitute for medical advice, official guidance or emergency services. Do not encourage sharing CNP, passwords, documents, home address or private medical information. The community CTA must describe its purpose truthfully.

## QA release rule
A new article must have independently documented source checks, ten editorial scores each >=8, image rights proof, and a fingerprint matching the published revision. Technical metadata tests alone never authorize publication. Failed review or missing proof keeps the article held. No automated agent may mark its own writing independently approved.

## Corrections contact
Until a verified public editorial contact route is documented, do not publish an invented email address or claim a monitored corrections inbox. Reports can be tracked internally through repository issues where enabled; do not ask readers to expose personal data in public issue threads.
