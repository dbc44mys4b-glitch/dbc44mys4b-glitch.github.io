Otopeni Airport: getting into Bucharest without the hassle

Just landed at Otopeni? Here’s how to reach the city, avoid traffic and find the right stop.

Train to Gara de Nord: skip the traffic

Trains between Henri Coandă Airport (OTP) and Gara de Nord run around the clock, roughly every 40 minutes. The journey takes about 20 minutes. Construction near the parking area may change the walking route to the station, so follow the current signs from Arrivals.

Bus 100: a direct city connection

Bus 100 connects the airport with central Bucharest. The approximate interval is 15 minutes during the day and 30 minutes at night. A standard 90-minute urban ticket costs 3 lei. Due to construction, the Arrivals (Sosiri) stop may be temporarily unavailable; check the Departures (Plecări) stop before travelling.

Taxis, luggage and meeting someone

Travelling with children or several suitcases? A car may be more convenient. Compare the final fare, check the vehicle registration and arrange a child seat if needed. Remember that OTP (Henri Coandă) and BBU (Băneasa) are different airports.
